What you get back for opening a CS2 case

Published drop probabilities times market prices, minus what it costs to open. Every figure is computed by this code from a dated snapshot. It measures opening a case, not holding one.

How it is calculated

Cost to open
case price + key price
Expected back
Σ (probability of outcome × its market price), after the seller fee
Return
expected back ÷ cost to open, minus 1
Chance of losing
total probability of outcomes worth less than the cost to open
Priced
share of probability that had a usable price. Outcomes with no price are left out, never counted as zero and never redistributed, so anything under 100% makes the return an upper bound and the chance of losing a lower bound.

Drop probability = rarity tier probability × 10% if StatTrak × the wear grade's share. Items in a tier are equally likely. Wear shares come from how many of each grade are actually listed; where too few are listed to measure, an even spread across the possible grades is assumed instead.

Where the numbers come from

Educational research. Not financial advice, not a prediction, and not a claim about what an unopened case will be worth later.
