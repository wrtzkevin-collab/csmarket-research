What does opening a CS2 case return in expectation?

A probability-weighted view of case outcomes, market prices and model uncertainty. Every result carries its source, observation time and data coverage.

Research preview Every figure below is calculated by this repository's pipeline from a dated market snapshot. It measures opening a case; it says nothing about the future price of an unopened one. Not financial or trading advice.
01

Gross return ratio

gross EV ÷ opening cost

The expected item value before seller fees. A ratio of 65% means $0.65 of expected item value per $1.00 spent.

02

Expected net ROI

(net EV − cost) ÷ cost

The expected percentage gain or loss after the modelled seller fee. Negative values indicate expected loss.

03

Observed loss probability

Σ p(outcome below cost)

The priced probability mass returning less than cost after the modelled fee. With missing prices it is a lower bound, shown beside coverage.

Method & assumptions

Gross expected value EV = Σ pi · vi

Each possible outcome value is multiplied by its probability. Net EV then applies the stated seller fee. Coverage reports how much of the outcome set had a usable price; it does not make missing values disappear.
